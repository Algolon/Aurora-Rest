## License guidance

1. CC0 is suitable for reference and potential texture sourcing, but independently assess resolution, provenance, quality and model release if needed; `CC0` is a statement from the referenced file's description, not global licensing of Wikimedia Commons.
2. The Hylocomium photo is CC BY-SA; original photos are intentionally **not copied or transformed into R03 art deliverables**. If later reused as any incorporated texture or derived asset, review required attribution and ShareAlike.
3. The original AI-generated R03 target images contain none of the CC0/CC BY-SA photographs as embedded source pixels. They are visual concepts, not botanical source evidence or exportable PBR material scans.

## Landscape-specific limits

A reference photograph of a plant from outside Scandinavia supports species morphology but does not establish what an Aurora Rest micro-site contains. Light exposure, water and soil type change moss/dwarf-shrub coverage. Density allocations remain **art choices for a fictional mixed birch/pine site**, not researched coverage percentages.
