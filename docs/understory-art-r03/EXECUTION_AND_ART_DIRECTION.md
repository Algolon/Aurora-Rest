### Visual hierarchy and scale

- **Walking-height plate:** eye approx 1.6–1.7m, forest floor visually dominates foreground, birch/pine tree context beyond. A winding narrow worn passage shifts naturally behind moss- and shrub-mounded borders. No dead-straight path, concrete-coloured stripe, tree rows or fantasy props.
- **30–80cm macro plate:** feathery *Hylocomium splendens* / *Pleurozium schreberi* moss with visible interwoven old-brown and new-green layers; leafy branching *Vaccinium myrtillus* with genuine light passing between shoots; lower, darker leathery creeping *Vaccinium vitis-idaea*; decomposed brown humus, dry pine needles, leaf fragments, micro twigs and occasional small lichen-dusted stone.
- **Material signal:** texture and contact transitions carry quality; moss must blend with shaded humus and fallen litter, not float like toy Christmas trees above a green sheet.
- **Typical morphology, NOT hard procedural targets:** *V. myrtillus* is a variable ~20–60cm deciduous dwarf shrub in woodland per Woodland Trust; in some sites much shorter. *V. vitis-idaea* is a creeping evergreen dwarf shrub. Feather moss forms loose interlocking mats in shade, often over humus and wood, with brown older tissue visible.
- **Colour palette (art-guide HEX; not approved PBR textures):** shaded moss `#465d36`, sunlit moss `#778548`, bilberry leaf `#64834b`, creeping evergreen leaf `#294c37`, humus `#392e24`, dry litter `#8f7653`.
- **Photoreal reference vs game style:** detailed imagery conveys botanical volume, material relations and natural irregularity; actual game can retain curated sculptural painterliness. Do not require photographic triangle counts or photo-derived maps to match the generated plate exactly.

### Gate / decision criteria

Only an independently reviewed future asset family could pass when all are visible from walking height and close-up: coherent foliage mass (not plain transparency), interwoven moss and soil, grounded contact shadows and organic texture, believable botanical scale, naturally legible footfall negative space, varied shaded and sunlit patches, no obvious regular lattice / billboard edge / tiled seam. Separate technical gates later: reproducibility, source rights, material atlas provenance, Android GPU performance, LOD, A/B/A restoration and protected source.

**Stop condition:** R03 is a completed 2D visual-specification milestone and must not start a new model or material micro-iteration. Only after owner selection may a separate milestone pilot ONE source-licensed moss/humus substrate + ONE convincing leaf-dense dwarf shrub silhouette, still offline from Birchwood until later explicit approval.
