## Independent art direction verdict

**Evidence of a good compositional principle:** The 11 irregular spatial colonies are easier to read than isolated specimens. Walking-height and overhead cameras now reveal useful contrast between moss-/shrub-led areas and the open serpentine passage. A1R removes the severe source-stage checkerboard/moire artefact.

**Not good enough for world art:** At real walking height, bilberry geometry still resembles widely separated leafed twigs, not full low dwarf-shrub mass; feathermoss is visually isolated over a flat green colour field, not a densely overlapping textured living carpet. Soil remains too smooth/brown, without natural-scale leaf litter relief, bark/roots, rock contact, tree-canopy shadow or a convincing world-context connection. The visibly authored dark footfall corridor is still schematic. This limitation stems from unapproved R01 source geometry/materials; merely tuning density and colours cannot solve it.

**Decision:** Keep this isolated A1R study as an authored landscape-composition reference, but ART REVISE and NO production adoption, merge, deployment, new asset-library registration, or Birchwood integration.
