- Finnish boreal site recommendations: field-layer dwarf shrubs can dominate and mosses/lichens comprise the ground layer, but composition depends strongly on site type. https://metsanhoidonsuositukset.fi/sv/standortsklassificering-i-skogsbruket
- Europe's Virgin Forests boreal overview: typical \`Vaccinium myrtillus\`, \`V. vitis-idaea\`, crowberry and mosses; sparse herb/grasses on acidic sites. https://virginforests.eu/boreal-region/
- North-European spruce forest community description: Vaccinium dwarf shrubs, feather mosses, moss-rich basal layer. The R02 site is a **stylized mixed woodland test**, not a claim that Birchwood is a canonical spruce forest. https://iprdatabase.eu/db/recent/sites/d4-north-european-moss-rich-spruce-forests-picea-abies-in-the-east-p-abies-x-p-obovata-p-obovata-with-dwarf-shrubs-and-herbs-vaccinium-myrtillus-vaccinium-vitis-idaea-trientalis-europaea-hylocomium-splendens-pleurozium-schreberi-dicranum-spp
