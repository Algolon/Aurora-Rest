## A — critical diagnosis before new geometry

1. **Subtraction destroyed the near field.** The original glade PNG retains a readable forest but almost no grass silhouette. Its rejected counterpart removes the canopy without supplying any ground hierarchy. Assets, not just tree counts, must connect the floor to the trunks.
2. **Camera geography defeated the sequence.** The turn faces an inclined textured floor beside a cropped trunk; the reveal looks over a near slope into sky/fog. Disconnected dry cameras did not establish a walk or an opening. Design from a contiguous canonical path and actual terrain sightlines first.
3. **The source representation limits cohesion.** Small isolated grass tufts disappear after the existing 19 m fade, bright convex rocks do not resemble mossy resting stones, and foliage silhouettes repeat. MAT has fine uniform texture without corresponding understory masses. This is partly a placement problem and partly an asset/material limitation. No material reclassification or density change is authorized here.

Six-dimension assessment: spatial categories currently blur into open woodland; tree clumps lack lower strata; the floor reads as a continuous textured plane; walking direction and anticipation lack landmarks; foliage remains stylized but floor and rock planes look provisional; original worker streaming, instancing, 22/36/62 m tree LOD and 19 m cover fade must remain intact.
