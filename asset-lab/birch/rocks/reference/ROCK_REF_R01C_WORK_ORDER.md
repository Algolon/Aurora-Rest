# ROCK-REF-R01C — Verified Natural Specimen Dossier (proposed; NOT yet authorized)
**Primary direction:** N01 Hunnebo klovor — weathered Bohus granite.
**Purpose:** replace "random low-poly plane design" with a photographed real-rock, multi-view natural-shape design baseline.

## Execution prompt
Act as a geological field-reference editor, Nordic nature art director, image provenance researcher, and Three.js asset QA lead.

1. Verify 3+ photographs from 1–3 **named specimens** of weathered Bohus granite, prioritizing SGU and real Hunnebo klovor references. Prefer one rock documented from multiple angles. Do NOT quietly substitute generic stock granite and claim it is Hunnebo.
2. For every image, preserve exact page URL, named location, credited photographer, known licensing, viewpoint, evidence level and relevant shape features. Keep files external until reuse rights are confirmed. No web-scraped photo rehosting.
3. Build a morphology breakdown of **large physical masses first**: silhouettes from front/right/rear/top where visible, 3–5 large uninterrupted surfaces, 1–2 small geological folds, natural joint termination and 2–3 contact points. Label assumptions vs directly visible shapes.
4. Compare with preserved G1 and ART-REJECT G02R. Set explicit "do not repeat" guardrails: no evenly sized triangulation, no clean mid-rings or bottom skirt, no symmetric dual peaks, no arbitrary groove, no visually uniform faceted sphere.
5. Produce a visual morphology board and one tight game-asset brief with target gameplay scale, visual readability at 5/10/20 m and plausible Nordic context. It should express natural continuous curvature and tectonic/weathering logic, **not** preselect a Three.js triangulation topology.
6. Recommend whether to pursue a manual art-directed 3D reconstruction, licensed photogrammetry input, or other image-based reference-first modeling approach. Include legal/use and runtime tradeoffs.
7. **STOP at photo and design approval.** No modelling, mesh asset, merge, world placement or deployment until owner reviews and selects reference specimen(s).

## Success definition
The owner can compare recognizable real stone exemplars and explain precisely which **natural shape**, not which generated mesh algorithm, the next Aurora boulder will reproduce and transform.
