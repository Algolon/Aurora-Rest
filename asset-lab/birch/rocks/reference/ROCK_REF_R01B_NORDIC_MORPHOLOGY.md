## 0. Mission and non-negotiable distinction
Produce 3–5 specific, authentic Nordic reference environments with verified geology and photographic resources; evaluate natural massing, weathering, fracture logic, material surface and ground contact; select one primary shape direction. Differentiate three evidence levels:
- **Lithological evidence:** rock type and regional geological history from NGU/SGU/GTK or other surveys.
- **Morphological evidence:** photographic or documented shape of an actual exposed rock or boulder at a named site. A lithological map does **not** encode a boulder's 3D geometry.
- **Art-direction inference:** a recommendation for Aurora; never assert that inferred angles, shape ratios, distances or detailed joint orientations were scientifically measured.

No photographic images are copied into this branch. Image copyright, licenses and reuse permissions must be checked separately before any asset import or redistribution; descriptive analysis and source links alone do not imply commercial photo reuse rights.

## 1. Prioritized morphology candidates

### N01 — Hunnebo klovor, Hunnebostrand, Bohuslän, Sweden
**PRIMARY SELECTED FORM GRAMMAR** · isolated weathered granite blocks and outcrops.
- **Official geological authority:** SGU (Sveriges geologiska undersökning), https://www.sgu.se/om-geologi/sevard-geologi/hunnebo-klovor/
- **SGU reference booklet with geologic field photos:** https://www.sgu.se/globalassets/om-geologi/sevard-geologi/geologiskt-arv/geologisktarv-2014-webb.pdf
- **Georeference:** SGU identifies 58°25'54.6"N, 11°18'4.9"E (≈58.43183, 11.30136) for the Hunnebo klovor geosite. These are **site-level**, not 3D-scanned boulder control points.
- **Lithology:** Bohus granite. SGU dates the granite to ~920 Ma.
- **Evidence-backed process:** intersecting vertical joints and horizontal sheet joints permitted deep chemical weathering along cracks. Subsequent glacial and meltwater removal of regolith exposed a mosaic of large rounded blocks and deep narrow clefts. Polished smoother walls higher up contrast with rough rounded stone low down.
- **Natural shape grammar:** coherent heavy rounded/asymmetric mass; 2–3 dominant irregular large surfaces separated by locally sheltered concavities; a few small transition faces concentrated around joint exits; compound rounded shoulders; genuine deeply embedded or natural block contacts.
- **Aurora transfer:** ideal first 2–4 m heroic forest boulder and grouped boulders. Prefer realistic asymmetric contour and local interrupted joint; don't replicate tall straight canyon walls as a small boulder.
- **Avoid:** uniform every-surface faceting, long perfectly straight notch, fantasy jagged peaks, equally sized triangles, cylindrical rounded cylinders.
- **Photo status:** official SGU web page and linked illustrated geological heritage booklet provide actual site imagery; prospective asset reuse **not licensed by this research**.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for lithology/process/site, HIGH for relevant macro-form, MODERATE for exact multi-view boulder profiles pending individual photo set.
- **Owner alignment:** Best match to the original G1's large-surface/small-local-fold contrast and his request for more naturally weathered stone.

### N02 — Kummakivi, Ruokolahti, South Karelia, Finland
**SECONDARY — SINGLE-BOULDER SILHOUETTE AND MASS**, not an appropriate generic ground-contact template.
- **Official park geological guide:** https://saimaageopark.fi/en/kummakivi-erratic-boulder/
- **Geopark/trail public photographic reference:** https://gosaimaa.com/en/kummakivi-erratic-boulder/
- **Additional Finnish Parks and Wildlife trail context:** https://www.luontoon.fi/en/trails/kummakivi-erratic-boulder-trail-ruokolahti-614914-en
- **Georeference:** Saimaa Geopark specifies 61.493320157 N, 28.430009592 E.
- **Lithology:** BOTH boulder and underlying roche moutonnée are mixed granite–cordierite–mica gneiss, according to the Geopark. Do **not** relabel Kummakivi as pure granite or confuse with rapakivi.
- **Evidence-backed process:** transported glacial erratic, approx. 7 × 4 × 5 m, exposed on another bedrock body and having ~0.5 m² contact area. Glacial grooves and chatter marks visible in the area.
- **Natural shape grammar:** one very strong contiguous mass, long irregular belly, partially weather-rounded corners, abrupt local planes, highly distinctive integrated silhouette rather than two assembled mini-mountains.
- **Aurora transfer:** useful as a silhouette and belly-mass study for one 2–4 m forest boulder.
- **Avoid:** copying the precarious balancing arrangement as generic natural support; it is a **rare individual landform**, not a typical embedded rock. Don't copy any peculiar tree growing on top as essential form.
- **Photo status:** official Geopark site and GoSaimaa photo(s); photo redistribution permission unverified.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for named individual boulder, geolocation, lithology and measured/estimated dimensions, HIGH for whole-mass visual reference.

### N03 — Ramsvikslandet / Tryggö, Bohuslän, Sweden
**SECONDARY — GLACIALLY POLISHED SURFACE FLOW**, different morphology from N01 although same regional granite.
- **Official protected-area description:** https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/vastra-gotaland/besoksmal/naturreservat/ramsvikslandet.html
- **SGU corroboration of Ramsvik bedrock/granite extraction:** https://resource.sgu.se/dokument/publikation/rm/rm108rapport/rm108-rapport.pdf
- **Visual reference photos:** https://www.bohuscoast.se/se/natur/ramsvikslandet-15998967 and https://www.utemagasinet.se/nyheter/20240520/9-aventyrliga-oar-i-sverige/
- **Site georeference:** Ramsvikslandet nature reserve and the shore from Sote huvud to Fykan / Tryggö, rather than an unverified exact specimen position.
- **Lithology:** red Bohus granite with locally other younger rock types (official county administration).
- **Evidence-backed process:** the reserve displays ice-shaped roches moutonnées, glacial striations, potholes and other glacial sculpting, especially Tryggö and the shoreline between Sote huvud and Fykan.
- **Natural shape grammar:** **long continuous swept surfaces** with broad curvature and flow, restrained shallow fracture channels, glacially polished convex shoulders and occasional harder stone edges.
- **Aurora transfer:** excellent for exposed coastal bedrock and surface weathering cues on boulders, not a standalone rock's volume by itself.
- **Avoid:** copying massive near-flat slab as a small round stone, or inferring that all smoothing comes from wave action; specifically document glacial action.
- **Confidence:** HIGH geology/process/reserve; MODERATE as source for freestanding boulder silhouette.

### N04 — Sörknatten Naturreservat, Åmål/Bengtsfors, Dalsland, Sweden
**SECONDARY — FRACTURE DIRECTION AND RESISTANT RIDGE MASSING.**
- **Government protected-area geology:** https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/vastra-gotaland/besoksmal/naturreservat/sorknatten.html
- **SGU regional Dalsland quartzite and brecciation context:** https://www.sgu.se/om-geologi/berg/sveriges-berggrund/landskapsstenar/landskapsstenar-i-gotaland/
- **Reserve photographic/trail references:** https://vastkuststiftelsen.se/naturreservat/sorknatten/ and https://www.vastsverige.com/amal/artiklar/ronja-rovardotter/
- **Georeference:** reserve between Djup and Ånimmen; a separately documented **southern parking/access point**, NOT one specific quartzite exposure, is approximately 58.877417 N, 12.476016 E (lakevanern.se reference).
- **Lithology:** quartzite of the Dal Formation and local schistose rock, with hard bare long ridges. SGU notes regional quartzite can be brecciated and quartz-healed in fault zones: do not assume that EVERY Sörknatten photographed ridge displays a mapped breccia.
- **Natural shape grammar:** long directional ridges and shears with large smooth/rough face transitions, natural natural gaps traversing a mass, growth of moss, heather, Scots pine in joints.
- **Aurora transfer:** best for cliff-adjacent talus, bare exposed rock and a rougher secondary boulder family in mountain foothills, not first forest hero boulder.
- **Avoid:** downscaling an entire ridge into an implausible miniature mountain.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for reserve/quartzite, MODERATE for individual fracture morphology and specimen attribution.

### N05 — Western Senja near Skaland / Trælen graphite-bearing metamorphic outcrops, Troms, Norway
**SPECIALIST — NATURAL BEDDING/FOLIATION AND CROSS-CUTTING FRACTURES.**
- **Official NGU deposit register:** https://aps.ngu.no/pls/oradb/minres_deposit_fakta_NY_KS.Main?p_objid=24697&p_spraak=E
- **Official NGU structural report:** https://static.ngu.no/upload/publikasjoner/rapporter/2003/2003_011.pdf
- **Official NGU regional units map portal:** https://www.ngu.no/en/geologiske-kart
- **Photo pointers:** deposit sheet has field photos of the area and folded graphite-rich schist, available through NGU site; keep credits/rights on source webpage.
- **Georeference:** NGU deposit registry point 69.497560 N, 17.203937 E. This locates a registered **deposit**, NOT a photographed individual fold or hypothetical Aurora boulder.
- **Lithology:** heterogeneous metamorphic region, including banded granitic and amphibolitic gneiss; mapped specific graphite-bearing schists and deposits. Do not attribute all Senja outcrops to the same lithology.
- **Evidence-backed process:** NGU 2003 report explicitly documents multi-phase F2/F3 folding, shear and brittle structures, which can produce non-parallel geological surface directions.
- **Natural shape grammar:** mixed-direction joints, folded/banded surface flow and occasional interrupted fracture line, distinct from homogeneous granite.
- **Aurora transfer:** valuable **secondary** mineral/foliation guidance for an intentionally metamorphic rock later, or mountainous outcrops. NOT an unverified boulder scan or a reason to add busy procedural stripes to N01 granite.
- **Avoid:** calling graphite-schist photographs granite; copying large tectonic folds at unrealistic miniature scale.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for geological processes, LOW to MODERATE for an isolated-boulder morphology until specimen photos are curated.

## 3. Primary selection: N01 — weathered Bohus granite of Hunnebo klovor
**Recommended form archetype:** *Weathered Nordic Corestone* (natural boulder). Keep The Sheltered Fault only as a design *motif*, not an instruction to force a continuous geometrical fault or pair of equal masses.
