## Recommended A: Nordic Weathered Corestone (owner approval pending)

**Shape:** one substantial, organically continuous irregular forest boulder, asymmetric high-left but *broad* crown, gently descending lower right/front lobe and one short sheltered discontinuous joint. Rounded and oblique surface transitions are allowed to remain large and quiet; small chipped edge surfaces cluster only at genuine fracture transitions. It has natural partially buried contact, not an inset radial foot-ring or a balancing pinnacle.

Provisional **game target** (not a measurement of either real stone): 3.0–3.8 m long/wide, 1.8–2.4 m visible high, 2.4–3.2 m deep. Do not force dimensions if the next owner-approved shape looks wrong.

- First-order **ONE** continuous body; forward convex asymmetric right belly is a continuous transition, not a separate small boulder.
- Second-order 3–5 broad naturally curving or locally planar zones: crown; broad slanted left/front shoulder; partially convex right/front belly; continuous rear sweep (inferred); sheltered local fracture transition.
- Third-order a small, interrupted, locally recessed natural joint with unequal weathered lips; 1–2 small edge anomalies only.
- Base partially buried with two or three broad ground-bearing zones as **Aurora art-design** rather than observed Myrstigen fact.
- Avoid: symmetrical twin mountain peaks; three stacked stone blobs; uniform triangular panels, low-poly sphere; fully straight deep fault; false black paint groove; constant bottom skirt; flat plate from side; exceptionally precarious Kummakivi support.

## Two variants (both owner-unapproved)

**B — Fractured Shoulder:** The exact same archetype and coherent overall mass, but a somewhat longer/clearer exposed localized joint and visibly more disparate opposing shoulder lips. Closer to Myrstigen. **Risk:** recreating the G01 fake central gorge if seam becomes continuous or dominant.

**C — Glacial Whaleback:** One long continuous belly with smoother crown and less prominent crack. Closer to the mass-reading of Kummakivi **without copying its perched contact**. **Risk:** anonymous polished low-poly mound lacking specific identity. Improve via controlled quiet-to-rough surface transition, not noise.

**Preferred:** A as midpoint between Myrstigen fracture and Kummakivi mass; B and C are legitimate owner alternatives. They are **not production assets**, and A is a recommendation, not an owner approval.

## Six view drawings: observed vs inferred

- **01 FRONT:** artistic projection using visible M01 break/shoulder motifs. Not a measured photograph; short upper-front joint and one lower forward lobe.
- **02 FRONT ¾:** **composed, not surveyed camera**. This is the preferred hero-review angle and must feel like one body.
- **03 RIGHT:** **inferred, not visible in Myrstigen references**; a deep continuous belly and broad asymmetrical rightward shoulder.
- **04 REAR:** **inferred**; quieter rear crown with nonuniform lateral sweeping surfaces, no fake copy of the front.
- **05 TOP/FOOTPRINT:** **inferred**; offset bean-like elongated outline, not an oval, neat rectangle or two blobs.
- **06 CONTACT:** **art-designed**; weathered broad partial-burial zones in nature context, not a reconstructed field measurement.

These views are *separately designed 2D silhouette studies*; they are NOT geometrically derived from a shared 3D surface. They demonstrate desired appearance, **not exact projection consistency or complete unseen geometry**. Retaining this caveat prevents false photogrammetric claims.

## Owner design acceptance gate

Judge each candidate WITHOUT granite textures and before implementation:
1. At 20 m, does its silhouette read as ONE heavy natural asymmetric body?
2. At 10 m, are 2–3 dominant major form transitions readable without a triangle pattern?
3. At 5 m, can we identify the short sheltered joint, unequal lips and coherent belly? Does the joint blend into mass rather than divide the stone?
4. Does the side/rear feel like the *same* stone without invented symmetrical peaks or a flat base skirt?
5. Does the visual language resemble a real weathered boulder more than G02R's roof-like/formal polygon construction?
6. Can the owner distinguish **photo observed features** from our **unseen backside assumptions**?

**Suggested decision:** accept A for later shape-blockout handoff, **conditionally** on owner confirming the short joint and visible belly are sufficiently natural. Alternatively select B or C or request one revised 2D art sheet. No automatic G02R2.
