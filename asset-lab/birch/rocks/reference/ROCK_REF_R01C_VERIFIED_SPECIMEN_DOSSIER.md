## 4. Evidence-grade morphology breakdown: M01 primary, K01/K02 supporting

### First-order mass and 2D silhouettes
- **Observed M01:** large contiguous oblique asymmetric rock outlines and a localized left-of-centre deep open joint; one front-right lobe much rounder than adjacent larger plane. Site vegetation conceals foot.
- **Observed K01/K02:** one broadly continuous large protruding body; no repeated tiny-peak skyline; broad curve with few darker broken pockets. Contact unusual and must be reinterpreted.
- **Design inference:** start with ONE heavy irregular boulder, NOT two equal lobes. Target visual width around 3.0–3.8 m, height around 1.8–2.4 m, depth around 2.4–3.2 m (game design targets, NOT specimen measurements). Allow deliberately nonuniform shoulders.

### Secondary large-area natural surfaces (3–5)
1. Broad, gently shifting crown (not roof slab, not paired pointed peaks).
2. One long oblique left/front glacial/weathered shoulder surface.
3. One convex and partially rough right/front projecting belly.
4. One or two shorter rear/side continuity sweeps **NOT visible in M01**, hence marked provisional rather than "reconstructed fact."
5. Local shoulder lip occluding a short joint opening.

### Local geological details
- **Observed M02/M03:** opposing crack edges do not share mirrored profiles, widen/recede irregularly, protected moss survives in the recess; neighboring convex faces look unequal.
- **Design inference:** fracture should stop in the surface and be occluded in parts. No uniform groove depth, black painted seam or open V from crown to base.
- **Possible weathering:** larger planar patches transition to subtly smoothed curves; the visual criterion is not 90th-percentile polygon area but surface-continuity and natural erosion directions.

### Ground contact
- **Observed:** M01 foot partly concealed by grass and moss; Kummakivi one exceptional ~0.5 m² balancing support.
- **Do NOT claim** exactly 2 or 3 support feet were observed at Myrstigen.
- **Aurora design target:** 2–3 broad asymmetrical ground-contact zones, partially embedded in trail-side grass/soil. These are an artistic extrapolation and need photo confirmation for exact spatial placement.

## 5. G1 and rejected successor-art crosscheck

**G1 strengths:** recognizable primary mass, multiple large stone planes, sparse localized breaks and asymmetric mass transitions; still stylized but visually authored.
**G01 failure:** radial fan triangles and fake shard fault.
**G01R failure:** clipped convex block and artificial notch.
**G02 failure:** uniform regular 6×4 surface scaffold, miniature dual mountain peaks, horizontal skirt.
**G02R failure:** intentionally unequal triangle areas but regular lower ring/large slopes; lacked real weathered joint and mass continuity.

**New non-negotiable:** organic forms arise from a real specimen's CONTINUOUS surfaces and erosion/joint logic, not from a target number or statistical distribution of mesh triangles. Triangles are implementation consequences, never the design.
