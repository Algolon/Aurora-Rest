## Non-negotiable silhouette
One 3.6 m wide × 2.4 m high × 3.0 m deep sculptural Nordic glacial boulder with an asymmetric high left/rear crown, a substantially lower right/rear shoulder, and a sturdy wide ground contact. Main mass ~75–80%; integrated shoulder ~20–25%. Front, rear, sides and top must differ.

## Geological structure
A recessed, irregular diagonal joint starts in the upper front-central stone and descends toward the right side, fading before ground contact. Its visible depth is roughly 0.06–0.18 m in normal zones and can reach ~0.25 m width locally. Express the fracture through actual displaced authored stone planes, not material markings or a black overlay. Interpret it as a weathered joint, not a tectonic fault.

## Authored geometry
Intentional crown planes, left shoulder, broad front plane, locally recessed joint, lowered right shoulder and independent rear-plane composition. Preserve a coherent 1st-order mass silhouette before adding sparse 2nd-order structural planes and almost no 3rd-order chips. **No low-poly sphere, convex-hull seed, copied G1 silhouette, Boolean union, attached slab, regular horizontal bands or random noise**. Target economical topology, not a triangle-count competition. Initial aspiration 130–220 triangles is NOT an acceptance threshold: shape quality wins.

Prefer one connected, closed external shell; if technical fidelity requires two solids, escalate before switching strategy. Maintain metre units, +Y up, +Z forward, ground-level pivot, finite normals, UVs, white vertex colors for existing A2 T1 material, and deterministic BufferGeometry.
