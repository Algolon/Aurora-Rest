## Focused mission and design guardrails
The owner singled out **the poor variation in surface scale and direction** as the most persistent defect of all successor rocks. Avoid the regular 6×4 G02 grid, similarly sized low-poly facets, twin sharp peaks, an open V ravine, vertical side skirt and arbitrary surface noise.

Design a non-convex rocky body with **authored unequal face sizes**, asymmetrically high/wide left-rear crown, integrated lower right shoulder, shorter tucked recess near the front and organically embedded ground contact. Do not add geometry density for its own sake.

## Implemented strategy
- New G02R source files only; do not overwrite even a byte of original G02/G01/G01R.
- Replace the repeated quad-cell topology, not merely their Y values, using independently authored 31 three-dimensional controls and 44 frozen, deliberately irregular top-surface triangular panel connections.
- Major structural features: multiple adjacent near-height main-crown controls create a **broad weathered plateau** rather than one spike; several lower, differently oriented eastern controls form the attached secondary shoulder.
- Short sunken frontal transition around x≈0.15 and +Z front, NOT a complete gouge bisecting two competing peaks.
- No continuous intermediate skirt ring. 16 irregular exterior edge anchors feed a foot that tapers INWARD; only five selectively placed local side relief controls interrupt the enclosing shoulder.
- Ground underside may use an invisible triangular fan; the geological visible upper surface never does.
- No runtime Delaunay, noise, convex hull, Boolean, generated spheres, new rock material or Feh Lu We dependencies.

## Technical and artistic gates
- Closed single oriented, genus-zero shell; deterministic positions, finite normals/UV/color, positive signed volume, no degenerate faces.
- Dimensions within roughly 3.6×2.4×3m. Measure upper panel 90th/10th face-area ratio; require >2.5, but do NOT treat this alone as proof of sculptural quality.
- Preserve exact G1/G02 reference geometry and existing A2 T1; use the same genuine Chromium WebGL2 capture views.
- **Required:** matched G1 vs G02R, matched G02 vs G02R (front 3/4 and rear 3/4), neutral clay eight views, wireframe, T1, context and metrics.
- Explicitly judge whether appearance is closer to left G1 without being a copy, and whether new plane hierarchy resolves equal-sized low-poly facets.
