## Execution objective
Make one fresh, artist-directed, non-convex blockout for The Sheltered Fault. The failure modes to avoid are G01's starburst triangulation and G01R's clipped-convex/house-shaped primitive. Maintain a high, broad left/rear main mass, a lower right shoulder, an irregular diagonal recessed joint and uneven supporting ground contact.

## Authored structural layout
Build a *single shared shell* from an explicitly hand-placed 6-column × 4-depth arrangement of three-dimensional geological landmarks. This lattice is a topology book-keeping scaffold, **not a generic noise-based terrain or repeating cube bevel algorithm**.

- Main stone: westward columns 0–2, two broad high crowns, y up to 2.4 m.
- Geological seam: column 3, four depth-dependent vertices diagonally shifting towards the right when viewed from the front. Front lip is recessed in Z as well as Y; depth fades toward rear stone.
- Secondary shoulder: columns 4–5, lower and oblique, with a broad flank and front plane.
- Side panels: irregular outer rim, intermediate battered shoulder and uneven foot. No uniform horizontal ring heights, no straight extruded slab.
- Target: ~3.6 × 2.4 × 3 m, stable ground pivot, one connected outward-wound closed exterior, one material.

All geometry is deterministic and manually authored. Two mass directions are compositional regions, not two overlapping spheres/wedges. No R04 coordinates reused, no Boolean joins, random noise, generic convex clipping, new framework, POP/WORLD/GEO/ENV/MAT changes or environment integration.

## First visual gate
Render genuine Chromium Three.js WebGL2 at neutral clay first: front, rear, left, right, top, front/rear 3/4, human height. Judge whether nonconvex crown reads as weathered stone rather than two miniature mountains, whether recessed joint stops before base, whether the rear/side has sculptural depth, and whether side profile is free of procedural bands. Do not camouflage failures with material.
